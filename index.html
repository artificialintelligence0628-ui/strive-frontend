Exercise 2 · Short Answer
Briefly explain the difference between transcription and translation in protein synthesis.
Short Answer
✓ Model Answer
Transcription copies DNA into mRNA in the nucleus. Translation decodes mRNA into a protein at the ribosome in the cytoplasm.

What is the significance of the semi-conservative model of DNA replication?
Short Answer
✓ Model Answer

Protein Synthesis
Protein synthesis is the process by which cells build proteins using information encoded in DNA. It occurs in two major stages.

The first stage is transcription, which takes place in the nucleus. RNA polymerase reads the DNA template and assembles a complementary mRNA molecule.

The second stage is translation, which occurs at the ribosome. The ribosome reads the mRNA in groups of three bases called codons, each specifying an amino acid.
